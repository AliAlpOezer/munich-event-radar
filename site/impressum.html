About the event data

Event Radar is a private, non-commercial project, not run by the Technical University of Munich. It collects events, dates and registration windows automatically from organisers' public pages, and some of them may be out of date or wrong. Before you rely on a date or a deadline, check it on the organiser's page linked from each event.
